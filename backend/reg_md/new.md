## Procedure to Get a New VIT ID Card
1. Obtain a letter from your proctor or Head of the Department (HOD).
2. Submit the letter to the Student Welfare Office (MGR Block, G15).
3. Pay ₹500 through VTOP.
4. They will issue you a new tag.
5. Collect the new ID card from the CTS Office.

## Placement Norms

| Company Selection | Eligibility for further placements/internships |
| --- | --- |
| Selected by any company for placements | Will be eligible for 2X CTC offer and aboveWill be eligible for Dream (Core) offer, if the CTC is less than 10 LPA or specified as “Dream(Core) |
| Selected for internship by a company (if the CTC on conversion is more than 10 LPA) or termed as Super Dream Internship | Eligible for companies (Placements / Internship + Placements) if the CTC is more than 2X and aboveEligible for companies(only placements) if the CTC is less than 10 LPA |
| Selected for an internship by a company if the CTC is less than 10 LPA or termed as Dream Internship | Eligible for companies (Placements / Internship + Placements) if the CTC is 2X and aboveEligible for companies(only placements) if the CTC isLess than 6 LPA |

**Note:**

1.  Super Dream(Internship), Dream(Internship), Dream(Core) will be specified in the placement notice
2.  Students who plan for higher studies are eligible to take only the IT Services companies offer (CTC – less than 6 LPA). **IT Services or regular offer** will be specified on top of the notice.
3.  Students dropping the Dream/ Super Dream (internship/fulltime) offer after selection, in any of the above categories will have to do one additional course and they will also be not be allowed for further placements
4.  Students withdrawing from internship before /after joining the company also will be considered as “dropping of offer”

**Violation of PAT Norms:**

1.  Students planning for higher studies are not eligible for Dream/Super Dream offers. They are allowed to attend only Software services company’s interviews (Like TCS, Cognizant, Infosys etc.). VIT will not issue “No Objection certificate” or recommendation letters to those students who get selected in Dream /Super dream companies and later plan to pursue their higher education. Dream /Super dream companies will be notified in PAT notices.
2.  Students who reject a Dream or Super Dream offer (Placement or Internship) after getting selected; have to do an additional academic course to complete their degree requirements. The academic course has to be completed in the final semester (no extension shall be given). The details of the academic course to be completed will be shared by the Dean (Academics) later. This also applies to the students who get Pre Placement Offers after the summer internships. The earlier rule of not getting the recommendation letter from the Professors for higher education also would remain. "Once the higher PAT/CDC offer is declined by the student, all the previous offers through PAT will also get cancelled/not considered"
3.  Only registered students will be allowed for campus interviews and other training programs organized by the placement centre. Those students attending campus interviews without registering and not fulfilling the eligibility criteria (as on date of selection test) would be summarily rusticated from the University for Bringing Disgrace to the University.
4.  It is found that Students who get placed in Dream or Super Dream are violating the PAT norms and attending the off campus placements. Students who get placed with on campus full time/internship placement (dream or super dream) must accept the offer without fail and cannot decline. Those who are going to get placed in Dream or Super Dream placements should follow the PAT norms. Students who are violating the norms will face severe disciplinary action.

**Note:**

All the companies allow students with no current backlogs for the selection process.

**PAT Category**

| Category | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Category I | 60 % in 10th, 12th, UG and PG as applicable and no year  gap in studies. |
| Category II | 60 % in 10th, 12th, UG and PG as applicable and upto 2 year gap in studies. |
| Category III | 60 % in 10th /12th /UG and more than 2 year gap in studies. |
| Category IV | Less than 60 % in 10th /12th /UG and upto 2 year gap in studies. |
| Category V | Less than 60 % in 10th /12th /UG and more than 2 year gap in studies. |

Gap in studies is counted from your Xth STD.
Work experience is also counted as an academic gap.

**Important Note:**

1.  **Registration is purely voluntary.**
2.  It is informed that students in category III, IV and V may not expect many companies as almost all the companies visiting VIT for Campus Placement insist on 60% consistency and less than 2 years gap (i.e. category I and II). Hence PAT registration is as per your discretion/decision only.
3.  All the companies allow students with no current backlogs / required credits completed for the selection process.
4.  Registration for other campus students will be done separately by their respective campus.
5.  N and F grades are considered to be backlogs by the industry. Hence, it will be counted as current backlogs.
6.  **Please note that PAT registration fee once paid is NOT REFUNDABLE.**
